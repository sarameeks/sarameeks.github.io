Bitlocker & Yellowkey Vulnerability Analysis

This project was completed during my job at my college help desk. I was able to work with another student employee and created a detailed beginner-friendly presentation highlighting the benefits, strengths, and limitations of Bitlocker. We then replicated and analyzed the CVE-2026-45585 "Yellowkey" BitLocker vulnerability on campus systems to evaluate detectability with various security software and its impact campus machines. Finally, we concluded out project with a proposed vulnerability mitigation and observed the effects of the mitigation.
